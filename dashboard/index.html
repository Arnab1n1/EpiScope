1. Incidence and alerts

Hatched = contains suppressed counts (1-4 cases hidden for privacy). Triangles = alert at the chosen threshold.

2. Weekly cases, forecast and alert threshold

Drag on the lower strip to select a time range (updates every view). Shaded bands: 80% and 95% prediction intervals. Dashed line: alert threshold.

3. Seasonality: week of year x year

Click a cell to jump to that week. Vertical marks: preparedness calendar (pre-position / campaign start) for the selected district's division.

4. Supply allocation

Bars per district: forecast demand (80th percentile, next 4 weeks), current stock, recommended allocation. Equity floor α = share of a population-proportional split every district is guaranteed.

5. Early-warning ranking

Ranked by C2 score at the end of the selected range. Click a row to select the district and disease.
